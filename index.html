  1. act is liable for suspension / dismissal.
  2. Students should neither involve nor encourage any act of boycott / strike / quarrels, etc.
  3. Students should follow dress code as prescribed by the University.
  4. Students should maintain punctuality and attend all classes regularly and on time.
  5. Students should not possess Mobile Phones in the premises of University Campus and Hostel. If found will be seized with penalty.
  6. Students should carry Identity Cards as long as they are in the University Campus.
  7. Students should wear uniform and be well groomed. Boys should tuck-in shirts without making noise.
  8. During lunch time students should take their lunch only in the allotted place / waiting hall.
  9. Students should cooperate to maintain cleanliness in the Campus. In this connection students are strongly advised to use dustbins.
  10. Students should maintain decency and decorum in the classroom.
  11. Students should not link or mess up with others items / cash / books / calculators, etc., in the class room or in the campus premises.

Students are strictly instructed to follow the above RULES and REGULATIONS.
